Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

This policy covers the GetMeThere app for Android (package com.getmetheregmt.app). GetMeThere is made by [developer name]. Questions: getmetheregmt.app@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There's no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that collects your data.
  • Your location is used on your phone to guide you. Your pins, paths and photos are stored on your phone.
  • A few features send limited data to other services, listed below. The rest happens on your phone.

Location

GetMeThere uses your phone's precise location to give directions, record the path you walk, drop a “GetMeBack” pin where you start, and show your speed. Guidance keeps running with the screen off or another app open, as a foreground service with a notification, so location is used in the background only while guidance or path recording is running.

Your location, pins, folders, recorded paths and photo pins are saved in the app's storage on your phone. The developer doesn't receive them.

Logs

To help fix problems, the app keeps log files on your phone, including locations and what the app did. A new file starts each day, and only the newest 30 are kept. Logs leave your phone only if you use “Send logs to developer”. Logs you send are used only to fix problems and are deleted when they're no longer needed.

Camera, Bluetooth and nearby devices

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo for a photo pin. The photo stays on your phone.
  • Bluetooth and nearby Wi-Fi devices: used for 📡 Find each other nearby and to connect to your own watch or accessory. They are not used to find out where you are.
  • Notifications: used for the notification shown while guidance is running.

Backups you make

💾 Back up everything saves one file wherever you choose, such as your phone, your Drive or an email to yourself. That file contains your pins, paths and settings, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Deleting your data

Since your data is on your phone, you control it. Delete pins and paths in the app (they stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, or empty it right away), or uninstall the app to remove everything. To have logs you sent deleted, email getmetheregmt.app@gmail.com.

Children

GetMeThere is not directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
